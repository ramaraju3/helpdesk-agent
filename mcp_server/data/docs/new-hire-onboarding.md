# New Hire Onboarding Guide

## Before day one
Accounts are created by the People team three business days before the start date, but they stay inactive until 6 a.m. local time on the first day. A message saying the account is "not yet active" before then is expected and needs no ticket.

## Day one checklist
Collect your laptop from the IT desk, sign in with the temporary password sent to your personal email, and enrol a second-factor device. You must change the temporary password within 24 hours or the account locks.

## Equipment requests
Standard kits include a laptop, a charger, a headset and one external monitor. Extra equipment such as a second monitor or a standing desk needs manager approval through the equipment request form.
