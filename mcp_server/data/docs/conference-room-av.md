# Conference Room AV Guide

## Screen sharing to the room display
Each room display shows a six-digit share code on its idle screen. Enter that code in the room-sharing app on your laptop. A wired HDMI cable sits in every room as a fallback.

## Remote participants cannot hear the room
Check that the ceiling microphone shows a green light; red means it is muted. Make sure the video call is using "Room Audio" as its microphone, not your laptop. Closing laptop lids prevents echo.

## Booking and room problems
Rooms are booked through the calendar. To report broken equipment, scan the QR code on the room's table, which opens a ticket already tagged with the room name.
