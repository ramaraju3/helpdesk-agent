# Offboarding Runbook

## Access removal
When someone leaves, their manager files an offboarding request at least five business days ahead. Access to all systems is removed at 5 p.m. local time on the last working day. For involuntary departures, access is removed immediately when the People team notifies IT.

## Returning equipment
Departing staff return their laptop, charger and badge to the IT desk on their last day. Remote staff receive a prepaid shipping box. Devices are wiped and reimaged before they are reissued.

## Shared files and mailboxes
Before access is removed, the manager decides who inherits the departing person's cloud drive files. Their mailbox is kept read-only for 90 days and then deleted.
