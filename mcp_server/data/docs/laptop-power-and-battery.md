# Laptop Power and Battery Runbook

## Laptop will not turn on
If the screen stays black after pressing the power button, connect the original charger and wait 10 minutes: a fully drained battery needs time before the machine will boot. Then hold the power button for 15 seconds to reset the power controller and try again. If the charging light never comes on, test with a charger from the IT desk to rule out a broken adapter.

## Swollen or hot battery
A battery that bulges, lifts the keyboard or makes the case rock on a desk is a fire risk. Stop using the laptop, unplug it, and bring it to the IT desk in person. Do not ship it. This is treated as a safety issue and handled the same day.

## Loaner laptops
While a laptop is being repaired, the IT desk lends a loaner for up to 10 business days. Loaners are wiped on return, so keep your work in the company cloud drive, never on the loaner's local disk.
