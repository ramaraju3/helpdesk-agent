# IT Incident Response Process

## Severity levels
High: a security incident, data at risk, or many people unable to work. Medium: one person or a small team is blocked with no workaround. Low: an inconvenience or a request with a workaround.

## Paging on-call
High-severity incidents page the on-call engineer for the affected area: security, network or workplace systems. On-call must acknowledge within 15 minutes. If there is no acknowledgement, the page goes to the IT manager.

## Communicating during an incident
For incidents affecting many people, the incident lead posts an update on the status page every 30 minutes until it is resolved, even when there is no news.

## After the incident
Every high-severity incident gets a written review within five business days covering the timeline, the root cause and follow-up actions. Reviews are blameless.
